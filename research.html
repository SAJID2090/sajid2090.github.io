Research

My research looks at how AI and spatial computing can give people on construction sites and in buildings the right safety information at the right moment. I work where computer vision, BIM and Mixed Reality meet, and I test my systems on real headsets rather than in simulation only.

Doctoral Research

Integrating Large Language Models with Visual Data to Automate Complex Reasoning in Construction Safety Diagnostics, funded by an NSTC Research Grant (Feb. 2026 to Present). I am developing an end-to-end AI inspection system that combines YOLOv8 object detection, Microsoft HoloLens 2, Trimble XR10, Unity Barracuda, C#, MRTK3 and the Claude Vision API for NFPA 10 fire extinguisher compliance assessment in construction environments.

Multi-User Mixed Reality Inspection Copilot

The core system is a HoloLens 2 and Trimble XR10 application that detects safety equipment with a custom YOLO model, overlays the results in the inspector's view, links findings to BIM data from Revit and AutoCAD, and adds an AI assistant panel for compliance reporting. The core research contribution is synchronising one inspection session across multiple users.

PCGML for Construction Safety

I apply Procedural Content Generation via Machine Learning to construction safety, generating varied site scenarios for training and evaluation. I started this line of work during my research internship at IIT Jammu, India, and have built a working prototype.

Immersive Safety Training for Construction Workers

I build construction site models in Plamo, export them as GLB into Unity, and use them as environments for immersive worker safety training.

I have created this website to highlight my academic and professional accomplishments, share my research projects and interests, and provide access to the systems and programming work I have built. You will also find basic contact information should you wish to get in touch with me.

Let's collaborate

I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Civil and Construction Engineering at the National Taiwan University of Science and Technology (NTUST), Taipei, Taiwan. My doctoral research, which integrates large language models with visual data to automate complex reasoning in construction safety diagnostics, is supported by an NSTC Research Grant. I build AI-driven, human-centred tools that bring computer vision, Building Information Modelling and Mixed Reality headsets together so that people on site get real-time safety guidance. I am always keen to work with research groups, supervisors, industry partners and labs on projects focused on, but not limited to:

  • AI for Construction Safety
  • Mixed & Extended Reality (HoloLens 2, Trimble XR10)
  • Immersive VR & AR Safety Training
  • Computer Vision & Object Detection (YOLO)
  • BIM-Integrated Digital Systems
  • Procedural Content Generation via Machine Learning (PCGML)
  • Human-Guided & Trustworthy AI
